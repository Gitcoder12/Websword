# Websword — Company and Team Fit

These are possible employer and technical-area matches, not customers, users, partners, endorsements or hiring interest.

## Project thesis

A browser tool that examines writing patterns and shows heuristic signals that may be associated with AI-generated text.

## OpenAI

**Relevant public areas:** Model evaluation, AI safety and reliability research.

**Potential roles:** NLP Engineer, Research Engineer, Evaluation Engineer, Applied Scientist or Frontend Engineer.

**Evidence to develop:** A static HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype that accepts pasted text and reports heuristic writing-pattern signals. It is not a reliable AI detector, cannot determine authorship, and must not be used for academic, employment or disciplinary decisions without independent evidence.
